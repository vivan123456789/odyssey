## The Short Version

We're a rotating Bay Area music collective available for shows 2–4 times per week throughout the summer. We play restaurants, markets, festivals, community events, and foot-traffic spots across SF, Oakland, Berkeley, San Jose, Marin, Half Moon Bay, and the Tri-Valley.

Every show includes a charitable component — a portion of proceeds goes toward a niche, high-impact initiative we select each season.

## What Happens When You Reach Out?

1. **Fill out the inquiry form** on our [contact page](/company/contact), or DM us on Instagram. Tell us your venue, preferred dates, and what kind of show you have in mind.

2. **We'll get back to you within a couple of days** to discuss availability, set size, and logistics. We'll ask about your space (indoors/outdoors, stage, PA situation), your audience, and the format you're looking for.

3. **We agree on the setup** — set length, configuration, sound requirements, and payment structure. We work case-by-case depending on whether it's a door deal, flat fee, or charity-driven show.

4. **We show up and play.** We're a collective, so the specific lineup may vary — we'll tell you who's performing as we get closer to the date.

## What Configurations Are Available?

We can scale from a small trio to a full 10+ piece ensemble with the complete horn section (sax and trumpet). Common configurations:

- **Trio:** Bass, guitar, vocals — ideal for smaller rooms and intimate settings
- **Five-piece:** Adds drums and keys — most common setup for restaurants and markets
- **Full ensemble:** Adds the horn section for festivals, outdoor stages, and larger events

## What Genres Do You Play?

The collective covers a wide range: funk, R&B, soul, jazz, rock, and pop. We also work in original material. Set lists are customized to fit the room and audience — we'll discuss what works for your context.

## Do You Need a PA / Sound System?

It depends on the configuration and venue. We'll discuss audio requirements when we connect. For some outdoor or larger shows we bring additional gear; for restaurant-style sets we often work with what the venue has.

## Anything Else?

For questions not covered here, [reach out directly](/company/contact) or find us on Instagram. We respond quickly and are happy to work through the details.
