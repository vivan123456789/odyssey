## What is [summer larp]?

[summer larp] is a Bay Area music collective built for the summer. A core team — Vivan, Abhee, Ayaan, and Shashank — assembled a rotating roster of over 20 musicians from the Tri-Valley and greater Bay Area to run a season of live shows with a clear purpose: raise money for high-impact charitable causes, and make it a great time while doing it.

The name comes from the spirit of it. A "larp" — live action role play — is a kind of collaborative performance where everyone commits to the bit. That's what this feels like. We're all playing different roles in different configurations, show by show, venue by venue. Except the cause is real, the music is loud, and the summer is short.

## The Roster

What makes [summer larp] interesting as a project is the scale of the roster. This isn't a fixed band. It's a collective — a pool of musicians drawn from based on who's available, what the venue needs, and what configuration makes sense.

On any given night you might see a five-piece with bass, guitar, drums, vocals, and keys. On a bigger night, you add the horn section: Rohan, Zafir, Sid, and Meng on sax, and Aga on trumpet. The sound changes depending on the combination, but the energy stays the same.

Check out the full [roster page](/roster) to see who's in the collective.

## Why the Tri-Valley?

Home base is Pleasanton, Dublin, and Livermore — the Tri-Valley. It's where most of the core team is based, and it's a community that doesn't always get the same live music infrastructure as the cities further north and west.

That said, the circuit extends across the Bay Area. SF, Oakland, Berkeley, San Jose, Marin, Half Moon Bay — we go where the venues and opportunities are. The Tri-Valley is home. The Bay is the territory.

## The Giving Model

Every show includes a charitable component. But we're not going to default to the same five organizations that already get most of the attention. The whole point is to spotlight niche, high-impact initiatives — the kind of organizations doing important work that a live music audience might not have encountered before.

We'll announce our charity partners as the summer gets underway. Stay tuned on Instagram for updates.
