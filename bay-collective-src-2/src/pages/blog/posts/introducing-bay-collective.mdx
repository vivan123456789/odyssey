This is Bay Collective.

We're a group of musicians from Pleasanton, CA who decided to stop just rehearsing and start performing — really performing, for real people in real venues, on a real schedule.

The Bay Area has incredible food, incredible neighborhoods, and a huge appetite for live music. What it doesn't have enough of is consistent, professional live performance at the neighborhood level. We're trying to change that.

## What we're doing

We're booking 2–4 shows per week at restaurants, downtown locations, and anywhere with foot traffic. We start on home turf — Pleasanton, Dublin, Livermore — and expand outward: Oakland, Berkeley, SF, Marin, and beyond.

Every show donates a percentage of proceeds to a charitable cause. We handle the finances transparently. We're not just here to play; we're here to do something worth playing for.

## The roster

We've got 15+ musicians across bass, guitar, drums, keys, saxophone, trumpet, and vocals. We configure the right lineup for each venue — a quiet jazz trio for a dinner restaurant, a full band for a plaza stage.

## Follow along

We're just getting started. Follow us on Instagram for show announcements, behind-the-scenes content, and updates on the circuit.

If you have a venue and want to talk, reach out. We'd love to play.

— Bay Collective
