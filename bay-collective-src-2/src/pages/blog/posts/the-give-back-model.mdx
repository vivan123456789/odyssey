Every show we play donates a percentage of proceeds to a cause we care about. This isn't a marketing line — it's built into how we operate from day one.

## How it works

When we negotiate a show, the charitable donation percentage is part of the deal. After each show, we calculate the donation, process it, and document it. We keep a financial ledger for every show: gross revenue, expenses, our cut, and the charitable contribution.

## Why we do it

Partly because we believe live music has a responsibility to give back to the community that makes it possible. Partly because it's just the right thing to do. And partly because it makes every show feel like it matters more — for us, for the venues, and for the audiences.

## Transparency

We take the financial and legal side of this seriously. Proper statements, proper invoices, full compliance. If you're a venue partner or just curious, we're happy to share the records.
