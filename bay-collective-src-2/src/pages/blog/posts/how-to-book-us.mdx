We get a lot of questions from venue owners and event organizers about what the booking process actually looks like. Here's the short version.

## Step 1: Reach out

Fill out the contact form or email us directly at booking@baycollective.com. Tell us about your venue, the kind of dates you're thinking about, and what you're looking for in terms of size and vibe.

## Step 2: We talk

Someone from our team will get back to you within 48 hours. We'll have a short conversation about your space, your crowd, your preferences, and what configuration makes sense. Restaurant dinner service? Jazz trio. Outdoor plaza? Full band with horns.

## Step 3: We figure out the arrangement

We're flexible on the financial structure. Flat booking fee, door split, tip-based, or something else — we'll work out what makes sense for both sides. We'll send a simple agreement and invoice.

## Step 4: Show night

We handle our own setup and break-down. We arrive early, run a brief sound check, and play. If anything comes up, you have a direct contact with us the whole time.

## Step 5: Give back

After the show, we process the charitable donation from the night's proceeds and document it. We'll share the record with you if you'd like.

We've worked hard to make this process simple and professional. If you have questions, just reach out.
